# High-quality instrument sample licenses

The files below are redistributed as compressed, mono MP3 derivatives for Freeza Live.
Their pitch and internal velocity relationships are unchanged. A uniform per-pack gain
normalization was applied; metadata, channel layout and codec were changed for reliable
mobile-browser decoding.

## FreePats Button Accordion HN

- Pack: `freepats-hohner-accordion`
- Project: <https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Organ/accordion.html>
- Source release: <https://github.com/freepats/button-accordion-HN/releases/tag/2024-03-29>

The bowed and plucked psaltery packs are deliberately labelled as tonal
approximations for the private `Erhu1` and `Pipa` program names. They are not
misrepresented as recordings of an erhu or pipa.
